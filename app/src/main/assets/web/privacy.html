Eastern Ethiopia Digital Broker ("the app", "we") connects people who want to buy, sell or rent property, vehicles and goods, and people who offer or look for jobs, in eastern Ethiopia. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

  • Account information: full name, phone number, email address and the account type you choose (owner, renter, job seeker, employer).
  • Content you post: listings (title, description, price, location, photos), job posts, job applications and CV files you upload.
  • Contact requests: if you ask a broker to call you back, the name, phone number and message you enter.
  • Payment records: brokers and administrators may record payments (amount, method, reference). The app does not process card or mobile-money payments itself.

How we use it

To run your account, show your listings and jobs to other users, let brokers and administrators in your city contact you, review posts for quality, and prepare aggregated business statistics. We do not sell your personal information and the app shows no third-party advertising.

Who can see it

  • Listing details and the contact numbers you choose to publish are visible to other users.
  • CV files are visible only to you and to the employer of the job you applied to.
  • City administrators, agents and the app owner can see information needed to manage listings, leads and payments in their city.
  • Investors with a viewer account see only aggregated numbers, not personal data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our service provider Supabase (cloud database and file storage) and is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your choices

You can edit your profile and delete your own listings in the app. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.
